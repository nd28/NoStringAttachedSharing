# Sharing: Effective Neovim setup for agentic coding towards 2027

**Video:** [Effective Neovim setup for agentic coding towards 2027](https://youtu.be/BhGWcQtq408)
**Creator:** [devaslife](https://www.youtube.com/@devaslife)

I'm sharing this video because it genuinely helped me, and I think it might help you too.

## Why I'm sharing this

1. **I like the aesthetics.** The whole setup looks clean and intentional — every piece has a place, and nothing feels noisy. It's the kind of environment I want to spend hours in.
2. **No talking, just ASMR.** There's no voiceover to follow; you just watch the screen and listen to the keystrokes and ambient sound. It's calming and easy to rewatch.
3. **It helps me set up Neovim.** Seeing a complete, working configuration laid out step by step removes the guesswork from getting Neovim ready for real work.
4. **I learn about new tools and configurations.** Each section introduces plugins, keymaps, and workflows I hadn't tried before, and I can borrow the pieces that fit my style.
5. **It's approachable.** For anyone who finds the terminology overwhelming, I've added a [`glossary.md`](./glossary.md) that explains the words used in this video and in Neovim in general.

## Before you watch

The video assumes you already know the basics of Neovim (modes, moving around, saving).
If that is new to you, read [`nvim-basics.md`](./nvim-basics.md) first — it is a gentle
introduction written for absolute beginners.

## How to use this

- Watch the video once without trying to copy anything — just absorb the flow.
- On a second watch, pause and recreate the parts you like in your own config.
- Keep [`glossary.md`](./glossary.md) open in a side pane when a term you don't know shows up.

## Notes

- This is one person's setup, not the "right" one. Take what serves you, leave the rest.
- If a tool mentioned in the video doesn't fit your workflow, skip it — the goal is a setup you enjoy using.
